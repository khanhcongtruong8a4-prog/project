   |q-qmid|<=0.01 rad and |qdot|<=0.02 rad/s throughout preparation are unproven.
2. Signed negative-physical-time execution support has not been implemented/validated.
3. Continuous-state neighborhood verification and real measured-endpoint check are missing.
4. The full 43-row QP was tested only in fixed midpoint/zero-speed fixtures, not at the
   actual post-hold state; complete A2 controlled execution is untested.
5. Policy v1.2.2 is a DRAFT. Observer semantics, startup, A2 reference and stop rules,
   independent clean-environment reproduction, theory/controls signatures and manuscript
   build revision confirmation are still pending.
6. Planner M1 bounds are missing. Moreover, the observer error floor still defeats the
   factor-two planner necessary speed condition at the nominal q for joints 1 and 3.
   Pre-settling alone is not a solution for Stage-1 planning at that point.
