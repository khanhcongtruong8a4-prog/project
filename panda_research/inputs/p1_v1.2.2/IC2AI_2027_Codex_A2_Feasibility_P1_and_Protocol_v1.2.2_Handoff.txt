PROJECT CONTEXT
The framework combines free-final-time, non-regenerative modeled-electrical-energy planning; Slotine–Li nominal tracking; a momentum disturbance observer; an online robust HOCBF hard-QP; and time-varying margins meant to establish planner/filter consistency. The selected seven-link Panda plant uses Gaz et al. inertial and smooth-friction data, with no additional payload or reflected rotor inertia:
  M(q) qdd + C(q,qd) qd + g(q) + F(qd) = tau + d(t).
The observer is p=M(q)qd, dhat=z+K_o p, with the manuscript's applied-torque z-dot equation. The current component code integrates z with q and qd in RK4, applies a zero-order-held filtered torque, and updates the controller/QP once per control period.
