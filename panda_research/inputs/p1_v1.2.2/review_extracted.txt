D. Midpoint QP infeasibility
1. Validity. For row i:
v_upper: −N_i(τ + b) + α_v(q̇_max − q̇_i) − Σ|N_il|ϖ_l ≥ 0
v_lower: +N_i(τ + b) + α_v(q̇_max + q̇_i) − Σ|N_il|ϖ_l ≥ 0
The sum, 2α_v·q̇_max − 2Σ|N_il|ϖ_l ≥ 0, contains neither τ, nor q̇, nor the controller or reference. So:
no torque of any size satisfies both rows, and torque limits are irrelevant;
ProxQP's PRIMAL_INFEASIBLE is the correct answer, not a convergence failure;
the condition is not specific to q̇ = 0. It must hold at every state along a run, with ϖ(t).
2. Root cause. The worst-case term Σ|N_il|ϖ_l is exactly tight for the box bound |d̃_l| ≤ ϖ_l. ϖ₀ = d̄ is the only bound available without truth, because d̂(t₀) = 0. What fails is the combination of:
the approved α_v = 5 and α₁α₂ = 25;
the full initial uncertainty, 2 % of τ_max;
the large entries of M⁻¹ at the midpoint (wrist inertia and coupling into joints 1 and 3).
The approved gains were selected (T14) without the G2.5 screen that would have exposed this.
3. Scope. What has been demonstrated is the approved q₀, ϖ₀ and gains, at t₀. ϖ(t) decays toward d̄₁/K_o. From the workbook's T10 values that steady level is about 7 % of ϖ₀, with roughly 0.15 s to 95 % settling. So the contradiction may disappear later in a run. That is unproven. This is not a global impossibility result.
