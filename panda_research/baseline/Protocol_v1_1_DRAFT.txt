A. SELECTED STUDY DISTURBANCE (T12/T13, PROVISIONAL FREEZE FOR MODEL DEVELOPMENT)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Adopt the dual-harmonic smooth time-only torque in v1.0 Section 4, NOT the
single cosine, 1%-limit proposal in the older action-pack workbook:

 d_i(t) = a_i [0.6 sin(omega*(t-t_ref)+phi_i)
                +0.4 cos(2*omega*(t-t_ref)+phi_i)],
 a_i = 0.02*tau_max,i [N m],
 omega = 1 rad/s, t_ref = 0 s, phi_i = 2*pi*(i-1)/7, i=1,...,7.

 dbar_i = a_i,  dbar1_i = 1.4*a_i*omega,
 including the 0.02 scaling in every bound.

This is a designed excitation, not a manufacturer disturbance measurement.
Use the same global time clock and phase across all methods and replans;
never restart the disturbance at replanning. The controller sees only the
analytic bounds, not the true waveform. Do not add undocumented noise or
state-dependent mismatch to the primary model-equality study.

Observer: p=M(q) qdot, dhat=z+K_o p, z(t0)=-K_o M(q0) qdot0 for dhat(t0)=0.
Choose varpi_i0=dbar_i (safe without knowing the waveform at t0). Gain
selection K_o remains T10. For replanning, retain z and the propagated bound.
These are development defaults; team approval needed to freeze for A3.

B. MARGIN MODE DECISION (PROPOSED TWO-EVIDENCE-LAYER POLICY)
----------------------------------------------------------
B1 -- Initial feasibility screen G2.5 only: evaluate optimistic local / point
    values and proposed gains to see whether dynamic tightenings are physically
    plausible. Mark estimates NON-CERTIFIED. A positive G2.5 is necessary to
    justify investing in verified interval bounds; it does not certify anything.
B2 -- Level 2 numerical comparisons: use numerically computed time-varying
    envelope/tube constants with documented sampling/interpolation, same
    method and time grid for all candidates, and label results SAMPLED.
    The baseline uses the candidate-wise maxima of EVERY matching envelope
    including chi_v, not independent arbitrary static reserves.
B3 -- Level 1 proof attempts: require outward-rounded verified enclosures,
    an independently checked positive global m1, verified tube/derivative/grid
    bounds, all-time residuals, and theorem initial/regularity conditions.
    Hybrid m1 certification via interval M-entry enclosures plus verified
    spectral perturbation is first route. Only the certified margin evaluator
    can support a Level-1 claim. The conservative 1/m1 inverse-entry bound is
    allowed but may make the planner infeasible; no automatic relaxation.
B4 -- The nominal plant and planner/controller evaluator MUST share exactly
    the same mapped dynamics and friction. Reusing constants from a different
    payload, rotor model, or frame map invalidates the proof assumptions.

NO algorithm may switch among these modes silently during comparison.
An evidence-level downgrade must be labeled and logged. The dynamic-vs-constant
baseline comparison must use consistent same-mode evidence and identical
model, task, solver policy, multistarts, disturbance and gains.

D. DOCUMENTED MODEL-ONLY IMPLEMENTATION ON 2026-11-01
----------------------------------------------------
The seven-body Panda model has been implemented using a single CasADi AD
source for M, dM/dq, g, U and flange FK; C is assembled from its AD derivatives.
The included Phase-3a internal numerical checks were executed and their exact
results are in phase3a_test_report.json. These are SELF-CONSISTENCY TESTS,
not formal verification of Gaz-to-Pinocchio inverse dynamics or Level 1 bounds.
The targeted source-level frame identity comparison uses separate NumPy MDH
and older franka_ros Xacro-origin kinematic implementations over 22 poses.

The strict manuscript equations are unchanged:
 M(q) qdd + C(q,qd) qd + g(q) + F(qd) = tau_applied + d(t),
 C_ij(q,qd) = 1/2 sum_k[ (dM_ij/dq_k)+(dM_ik/dq_j)
                             -(dM_jk/dq_i)] qd_k,
 Mdot(q) = C(q,qd) + C(q,qd)^T,
 tau_ID = M qdd + C qd + g + F.
