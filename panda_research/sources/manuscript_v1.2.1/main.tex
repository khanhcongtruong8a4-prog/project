\begin{abstract}
A time-energy optimized robot trajectory may require torque corrections when disturbances, tracking errors, or safety filtering affect execution. This paper couples free-duration quintic B-spline planning with a momentum disturbance observer, Slotine-Li tracking, and a robust high-order control barrier function quadratic program (HOCBF-QP). Time-varying margins constructed from observer, tracking, and torque-residual envelopes tighten joint-position, joint-speed, torque, and manipulability constraints. Under validated uncertainty bounds, a verified tracking tube, and positive continuous-time reserves, the robust QP returns the nominal tracking torque unchanged while preserving all stated safety and actuator limits. For infeasible hard-QP instances, a braking-oriented soft-QP provides torque-bounded recovery and triggers replanning, without the consistency guarantee or barrier-invariance claim.
\end{abstract}
