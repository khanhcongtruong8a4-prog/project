# Frozen baseline and hypothesis contract

The exact JSON is authoritative. Reproduce four historical active E3 C4 cells
and all19 frozen A2 cells on an unmodified checkout, retaining every failure.
All original metrics, grids, reference, gains, guard and thresholds are fixed.
The working hypothesis is a moving-face local truncation defect. Its scope
must be derived, not inferred from a fitted order. First-order behavior alone
does not establish failure of every controller or of finite tolerances.

Read-only operator ablations, electrical quadrature and event/stage residuals
are diagnostic. Unsafe hypothetical extrapolations never actuate. Stage/node
checks are not a verified continuous-time enclosure. The separate alpha10
challenge time-compresses the same path to0.5s to exercise active filtering;
it is a harder new test, not a changed historical reference or gate.

No new runtime candidate is selected until the derivation supports it. Existing
post-filter/projected-feedback AB2 failures remain rejected. A changed accuracy
or hold policy is a separately versioned proposal; adoption remains contingent
on owner authorization. This is not a request for another administrative
signature cycle for authorized diagnostic work.
