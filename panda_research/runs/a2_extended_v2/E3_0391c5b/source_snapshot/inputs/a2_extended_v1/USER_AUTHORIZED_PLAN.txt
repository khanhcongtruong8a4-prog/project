IMPORTANT DIGITAL-SAMPLER DISTINCTION
The continuous Slotine-Li request r_k is not the same as the digital request
presented to the QP. The r4 policy defines:
    u_0 = r_0,
    u_k = r_k + 0.5 (r_k - r_(k-1)) for k >= 1,
    tau_k = QP(q_k, qd_k, observer_k, u_k).
Only tau_k is applied and held, and exactly tau_k feeds the observer.
The existing reports claim max |u-r| about 2.81e-3 N m and max |tau-u|
about 3.81e-9 N m in the original short test. These are BASELINE REPORTED
values, not new results or bounds. Always report three separate quantities:
    (1) digital sampling correction: u - r,
    (2) filter intervention: tau - u,
    (3) total applied-vs-continuous difference: tau - r.
Do not conclude 'zero intervention from the continuous Slotine-Li request'
solely because ||tau-u|| is small. Do not change the continuous-time theorem or
claim digital implementation inherits its guarantees without new analysis.
